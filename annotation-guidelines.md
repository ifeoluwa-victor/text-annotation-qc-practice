# Annotation Guidelines

**Task:** Read each short text and give it ONE label that describes what the person mainly wants.

I label the type of question only. I do not answer the questions.

## Labels
| Label | Use when the text asks for... | Example |
|---|---|---|
| DEFINITION | the meaning of a term, or an explanation of why or how something works | "What is a transducer?" |
| PROCEDURE | how to carry out a task or technique | "How do I measure crown-rump length?" |
| SAFETY | safety, risk, radiation protection, or the wellbeing of a patient or staff member | "Is it okay to skip radiation protection training?" |
| STUDY_CAREER | studying, books, exams, jobs or scholarships | "Where can I find free anatomy quizzes?" |
| UNCLEAR | something off topic, too short, or impossible to tell | "help" |

## Rules
1. Choose the main thing the person wants, not a side detail.
2. If the text is about safety, radiation risk or a person's wellbeing, use SAFETY, even if it also asks how or what.
3. Use UNCLEAR only when no other label fits.
4. Do not guess hidden meaning. Label only what the text says.
5. Add a short note whenever the choice was hard.

## Unclear cases and how I handle them
| Text | Why it is hard | Label | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Why is gel used during an ultrasound scan?" | Could be about a procedure or a concept | DEFINITION | It asks why, which is an explanation |
| "Is ionizing radiation used in ultrasound?" | Looks like a fact question | SAFETY | It is about radiation, so rule 2 applies |
| "What do I do if a patient feels dizzy during a scan?" | Could be a procedure | SAFETY | It is about a patient's wellbeing, so rule 2 applies |
| "Can AI tools help me study radiography?" | Could be a general technology question | STUDY_CAREER | The main purpose is studying |
